FAQ

Questions, answered

Before you ask

Do my guests need to install an app?+
No. Guests scan the QR with their phone camera and the menu opens in the browser. No download, no account, no login. It just works.
What happens if the wifi drops mid-service?+
The kitchen screen keeps showing the tickets it already received. Once the connection returns, any orders placed during the outage sync automatically. Nothing is lost.
Can I upload my existing menu?+
Yes. Export your menu to Excel, upload it, and we map the columns for you. Most outlets are live within an evening.
Do you take a cut of each order?+
Never. You pay a flat monthly fee. Every rupee a guest pays goes to you — no commission, no hidden percentage, no surprise.
How long does setup take?+
Our team switches you on by hand. From uploading your menu to printing your first QR, most restaurants are live in under a day.
Can I run multiple outlets on one account?+
Yes. One login covers every outlet. Switch between branches from the dashboard without logging out or losing your place.
